REXONA BRAND DNA — Extract for Kairos BrandDNAi Grounding
(Source: public Rexona/Sure/Degree brand guidelines, Unilever brand book excerpts)

============================================================
1. BRAND PROMISE
============================================================
"It won't let you down."
Rexona is the anti-perspirant that gives people the confidence to move more —
whether that is a job interview, a first date, a marathon, or a match-defining
sixth ball. The brand exists to remove sweat as a source of hesitation.

============================================================
2. TONE OF VOICE
============================================================
- Confident, never boastful
- Warm, human, sport-adjacent — but not tribal or aggressive
- Uses movement verbs: push, run, lift, dance, dare, go
- Avoids shame-based language around sweat: sweat is proof of effort, not a defect
- Global tone; culturally adapted per market (see localization rules)

============================================================
3. VISUAL IDENTITY
============================================================
- Primary color: Rexona blue (#0038A8 approx)
- Secondary: white, silver, active reds/oranges for men's variants
- Photography: real people mid-motion, real sweat visible, warm natural light
- Never overly airbrushed or "clinical"

============================================================
4. BRAND SAFETY — HARD DO-NOTS
============================================================
- Do NOT use content that shames body odor or sweat
- Do NOT mock athletes, officials, or public figures
- Do NOT infringe on league IP (no team logos, no player names without rights,
  no broadcaster marks)
- Do NOT use content that could be read as gender-, race-, or body-shaming
- Do NOT use content depicting minors in sweat/deodorant contexts
- Do NOT associate with alcohol, tobacco, gambling, or political messaging
- Do NOT use religious symbols as marketing vehicles

============================================================
5. CULTURAL MOMENT PLAYBOOK
============================================================
Rexona has permission to enter a cultural moment ONLY when:
  (a) The moment involves visible physical effort, pressure, or performance
      (sport, dance, work, first-time experiences)
  (b) The brand can add to the moment without interrupting it
  (c) The creative celebrates the person under pressure, not the brand itself
  (d) The brand name/logo appears organically, not stamped on top

Approved moment archetypes:
  - Athletes mid-effort (any sport)
  - Officials/referees under scrutiny
  - Performers before going on stage
  - Public speakers, interviewees, first-day-of-work moments
  - Dancers, especially in high-heat/high-energy formats

============================================================
6. MARKET-SPECIFIC NOTES
============================================================
INDIA (Rexona):
  - Cricket dominates; football is rising in Kerala, West Bengal, Northeast
  - Use Hindi + English mix ("Hinglish") for Gen Z; regional languages for tier 2+
  - Modesty norms: no bare underarms without context; sport context makes it OK
  - Price point matters: emphasize value ("24-hour protection at everyday price")

BRAZIL (Rexona):
  - Football is universal; also Carnival, funk, capoeira
  - Warm, expressive tone; humor lands well
  - Body-positive across all body types is table stakes
  - Portuguese language, Brazilian slang OK ("suar a camisa" = "sweat the shirt")

UK (Sure):
  - Football, rugby, cricket; also nightlife, gigs, festivals
  - Dry humor works; earnestness can feel forced
  - "Sure" not "Rexona"; do not use the Rexona name in UK creative

US (Degree):
  - NFL, NBA, MLB, plus emerging: pickleball, F1, women's sports
  - "Degree" branding; motivational tone; Team USA/Olympic association strong

INDONESIA (Rexona):
  - Badminton, football, motorsport
  - Bahasa Indonesia; Muslim-majority — modest visuals, no alcohol context
  - Ramadan/Eid moments high-priority; back-to-work post-Eid = key window

============================================================
7. REGULATORY / LEGAL FLAGS
============================================================
- India: DPDP Act 2023 compliance for any first-party data capture
- EU: GDPR, DSA — creator ads must carry #ad disclosure
- KSA/UAE: religious-content pre-clearance required
- Brazil: LGPD; ANVISA rules on efficacy claims ("24-hour" needs test data)
- US: FTC endorsement guides — #ad, #sponsored required on creator posts
- All markets: no medical/dermatological claims without evidence dossier

============================================================
8. APPROVED MOMENT ATTRIBUTES (scoring inputs)
============================================================
Brand-fit score components (each 0–100, weighted):
  - Effort visibility (25%): is physical effort visible in the moment?
  - Pressure context (25%): is the subject performing under stakes?
  - Positive framing (20%): does entering the moment celebrate the person?
  - Safety clearance (20%): no legal/cultural/IP risk?
  - Reach potential (10%): velocity × audience overlap with Rexona buyers?

Auto-reject triggers:
  - Any political, religious, or ideological content
  - Any moment involving injury, death, or public tragedy
  - Any moment where humor at a person's expense is the driver
  - Any moment with sentiment >20% negative in first hour
